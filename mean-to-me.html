III · What you mean to me

What you mean to me.

Are you okay?
I’m fine.
No, you’re not.

You notice when I’m not okay, even when I try to pretend that everything is fine.

Our conversations have become the highlight of my day: the gist, the laughter that follows, and then sharing our problems and somehow knowing just how to solve them.

You have helped me grow, too.

You taught me that being corrected doesn’t necessarily mean being attacked.

That taking accountability doesn’t diminish one’s worth.

And that two people can disagree without making enemies of each other.

You helped me understand that sometimes the most loving thing someone can do is hold up a mirror to you, and allow you to see the things you might otherwise overlook.

I appreciate that you don’t always tell me what I want to hear, but you’re willing to tell me what I sometimes need to hear. I hope you know I don’t take that for granted.

There’s a little more.
