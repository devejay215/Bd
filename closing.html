IV · From me, to you

From me, to you.

Clare,

Not long ago, you were telling me how heavy things felt. The transfer that didn’t work out, the expenses you were struggling with. I told you to be grateful that you’ve never gone broke, that you can still take care of yourself, and not to judge everything by one hard season. I told you that you can get through it if you put your mind to it.

And for the first time in your life, you agreed with me and didn’t argue. I’m still recovering. 😂

That’s who you are. You feel everything, you let it show, and then you get up again. All you ever need is a little encouragement.

You tell me you love me, and you show me you mean it. I know you have your own life and your own battles, and I can’t carry them for you. But I can be your peace, and your support.

This year, I hope you find calm. I hope what happened last year loses its hold on you, and that good things find their way back to you. May joy come easily, may your mind be at peace, may your work be seen, and may you grow into the best version of yourself, though from where I stand, she’s already here.

A prayer for you

My prayer for you is that God continues to guide your steps and order your path in ways that exceed your expectations. I also pray that God blesses the work of your hands, grants you wisdom and surrounds you with people whose intentions are as beautiful as their words.

May doors open for you in places you never imagined, and may you receive answers to prayers you haven’t yet found the words to say.

May God protect your peace and give you countless reasons to be grateful for the life you’re living.

All in sound health.

THE ARRANGEMENT

Before you start behaving like some heavenly creature who has descended upon the earth… I have grown far too attached to you, and returning you to where I found you is no longer an option. You are stuck with me, and honestly, I’m glad, because you feel like home. You will have to endure my random thoughts, my questionable decisions and my occasional madness, and I promise to love you through every one of yours. I hope you have made peace with this arrangement, because I certainly have no intention of terminating our contract. 😌

Clare
