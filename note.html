I · How it began

A little note for you.

Clare,

I still remember the day we met in the Mass Communication department. You were so full of life. You walked up, took my phone and said you wanted to make videos. I don’t usually talk to my coursemates, but I gave it to you anyway.

Then we went back to our separate lives. Exams brought us back together, reading side by side, and I told myself that was all it was. But the holiday came, and you called to check on me. I was surprised. I never thought I mattered beyond the exams, and you showed me I did.

When we resumed, I came to your lodge and you fed me. We walked to class together. It became our routine, and somewhere inside that routine, you became someone I can’t imagine my days without.

I’ve also seen you in your hardest moments. You feel everything deeply, and you don’t hide it when you’re frustrated. I have never seen that as weakness. A little encouragement is all it takes, and you rise again. That is a rare kind of strength, the honest kind.

Happy birthday, Clare.

With love,

Obiamaka

Yes, Obiamaka. The name you use when you want to annoy me.
